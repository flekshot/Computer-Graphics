## Texture attribution

Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Earth day/night/clouds, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Saturn ring alpha, Uranus, Neptune, Sun, Moon and Milky Way textures were created/distributed by **Solar System Scope / INOVE**. They are used under **CC BY 4.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The source collection is https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/ . Individual URLs and checksums are in `assets/textures/SOURCES.json`.

Downloaded image files are unchanged. Runtime lighting, cloud compositing, bloom, tone mapping and gamma correction alter their displayed appearance. Procedural missing-texture fallback images are generated by this project's code and are not substitutes claimed to be NASA observations.

The texture provider explains that the maps use NASA elevation and imagery data, with artistic adjustments and fictional terrain in unmapped areas. Neither NASA nor the texture authors endorse this project.
